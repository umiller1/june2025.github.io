Chicken Pot Pie for Two

Prep Time: 15 Minutes Total Time: 45 Minutes

Cook Time: 30 Minutes Yields: 2

Packed with flavor, this is not your momma’s chicken pot pie. It’s creamy, herby, and loaded with tender chicken and veggies, all tucked under buttery puff pastry squares that bake up golden and crisp. Perfect for the colder nights coming up, it’s cozy, comforting, and the kind of dinner you’ll want on repeat all season long.
